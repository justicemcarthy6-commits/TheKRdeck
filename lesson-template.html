Discover the amazing biology of one of the forest's most colorful flyers!

About This Lesson

In this KRKIDS episode, we explore the canopy to find the Great Blue Turaco. Watch the animated sequence to learn about their unique crests, their specialized toes, and how they navigate their environment.

Scene Highlights:

  • Scene 1: Introduction to the dense forest canopy.
  • Scene 2: The Turaco's unique diet and digestion.
  • Scene 3: Flight mechanics and jumping abilities.
